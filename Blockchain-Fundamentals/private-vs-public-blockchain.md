# Public vs Private Blockchains

## Overview

Blockchains can be categorized based on who is allowed to participate and validate transactions.

The two main types are Public and Private blockchains.

---

## Public Blockchains

Public blockchains are open to anyone.

Examples include Bitcoin and Ethereum.

### Characteristics

- Permissionless
- Transparent
- Decentralized
- Anyone can validate transactions

### Advantages

- High security
- Trustless environment
- Resistant to censorship

---

## Private Blockchains

Private blockchains are controlled by a specific organization.

### Characteristics

- Permissioned access
- Limited validators
- Controlled governance

### Advantages

- Faster transactions
- More privacy
- Better suited for enterprise use

---

## Key Differences

| Feature | Public | Private |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Open | Restricted |
| Control | Decentralized | Centralized/Consortium |
| Speed | Slower | Faster |

---

## Key Takeaways

- Public blockchains prioritize decentralization.
- Private blockchains prioritize efficiency and control.
- Both serve different use cases.
