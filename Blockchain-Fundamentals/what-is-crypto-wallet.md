# What is Gas in Ethereum?

## Definition

Gas is the fee required to perform transactions or execute smart contracts on the Ethereum network.

It compensates validators for computation.

---

## Why Gas Exists

Gas prevents:

- Network spam
- Infinite loops in smart contracts
- Resource abuse

---

## How Gas Works

Transaction Cost = Gas Price × Gas Used

Factors affecting gas:

- Network congestion
- Transaction complexity
- Demand for block space

---

## Example

Sending ETH uses less gas than interacting with a complex DeFi protocol.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Gas pays for computation.
- Higher demand increases fees.
- Efficient contracts reduce gas usage.
